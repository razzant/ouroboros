# Review & Commit Protocol

This chapter owns preflight, review, publication binding, paid-cycle reuse and release sync. Technical failure and commit permission are separate facts: missing review never becomes PASS. `docs/CHECKLISTS.md` owns reviewer questions, severity and output contracts; ARCHITECTURE §6 "Git and commit review" and "Review stack" own the mechanism.

Finish the edits and focused tests, then call `commit_reviewed`; `preflight_reviewer` (or standalone `preflight_review(reviewer=…)`) buys one named catalog row's earlier look. `commit_reviewed` lands only in the system repository (any other `root` is a typed `TOOL_ARG_ERROR`); another registered root is reviewed with `review_change` and committed there with ordinary git (ARCHITECTURE §6 "Change review on any root"). Enforced by `tests/test_commit_gate.py` and `tests/test_review_change_end_to_end.py`.

1. **Prepared preflight.** Authorization and unresolved-work checks come before file preparation, staging and the fingerprint; free-cycle and budget admission come before any paid look. The free deterministic checks and the tests preflight run on every commit; a named row then looks once, inline with the full `review_rebuttal`, and only informs (`skip_advisory_review=True` records `preflight: skipped`, no name `not_performed`: facts, not audited bypasses). A free replay buys no wave, and an explicit test skip is not green proof (ARCHITECTURE §6 "Commit preflight").
2. **Review and author response.** Blocking keeps its one-call PASS. Every Advisory or Cyber Pro outcome, PASS and pending, returns before Git; Main may finish unchanged, correct, request another panel, or stop. Free continuation passes `review_reference` with `author_disposition` and rationale (optional `subagent_check` is `review_replaced_by_subagents`, not a seat or PASS), binds current bytes and reruns required checks without another critic. Blocking needs reviewer approval (ARCHITECTURE §6 "Git and commit review").
3. **Publication binding.** The created commit or tag is checked against that same binding before push; a mutation, rebase, conflict resolution or changed landing parent needs a fresh current-candidate binding under the applicable critic or Advisory author authority, with the same checks.

Keep optional task evidence after the stable governance prefix and shrink it before any existing review material; a source pointer gives a packet-only row no retrieval; removing an ignored view never deletes the canonical source (ARCHITECTURE §6 "Commit review evidence").

### Ledger honesty and failure

Each wave or free pre-dispatch refusal leaves an `ouroboros/review_ledger.py` record, linked to outcome and attempt by `review_record_id` (ARCHITECTURE §6 "Review ledger record"). Keep no `PASS` without a usable answer; $0 refusals `NOT_DISPATCHED`; open custody `pending`; same-attempt settlement raises `revision`; unobserved models `unknown`. The record informs, never authorizes. Keep reported zero/partial and absent cost separate from status and billing finality. Checkpoint failures retain primary acquisition and cleanup causes as local authority errors in the existing diagnostic carrier. Tests: `tests/test_review_ledger.py`, `tests/test_review_cost_knowledge.py`, `tests/test_review_checkpoint_cause.py`.

A technical review failure keeps its phase, reason, received findings and full result recorded as failure, never PASS. Once its typed outcome reaches Main it may support explicit Advisory author continuation on a known, independently bound candidate; outside Cyber Pro, Blocking still blocks. Cyber may continue without a new review, keeping the original findings and pending invocation; it never fabricates candidate bytes, custody or physical effects, and Stop, deadline and cost facts stay recorded.

Named `review_change` owns no commit custody or panel verdict. Prepared/lost reconciliation preserves the index, never restages it; same-candidate/intent retries collect original paid seats, never replacements (ARCHITECTURE §6 "Late completion and typed refusals"). Keep full raw results, PASS and empty-clean responses. Contributor checks accept exact IDs or bound catalog proof (§6 "Session identity"); labels alone stay unverified. Other refusals keep their causes.

### Review pool and paid cycles

The review pool is the enabled `review_eligible` rows of `OUROBOROS_SUBAGENTS`; effort and delivery are row properties, duplicate model ids stay independent seats, and `config.adaptive_quorum` owns quorum. Blocking seats every pool row, Advisory the named rows (with a reason); Cyber Pro composes; unmarked additions advise, never count. Every seat receives one brief — the change question, plus on every retrieving seat the repository-wide coupling question; there is no separate scope role (ARCHITECTURE §6 "The coupling question (Part 2 of the brief)"). Window size neither grants nor removes authority. Reading coverage is diagnostic on every route: keep it beside the verdict; never let it exclude a responding reviewer from quorum, block a commit or buy another review — the author judges whether a concrete gap needs more reading. Failed delivery or brief assembly is never a clean verdict. A retrieving delivery canonicalizes by output shape (`triad_review.review_output_shape`: `array` | `object` | `report` | `two_part` | `plan_reply`), never by surface name; a new surface registers in the shape table.

Paid review cycles across every gate share one owner knob, `OUROBOROS_REVIEW_MAX_CYCLES` — a string: a positive integer or `unlimited`; a malformed value fails closed to the shipped default. Its SSOT is `ouroboros/review_cycles.py`; the three per-gate meanings (plan asks stand outside it) live in ARCHITECTURE §6 "Review stack", the default in §7 "Default settings". `unlimited` removes only the local count; deadline, budget and lifecycle rails still bind. The paid fact is stamped write-ahead at physical dispatch, so a pre-transport refusal — panel assembly, an unavailable route, a money refusal — consumes nothing and stays retryable (ARCHITECTURE §6 "Paid stamp and owner custody").

Never pay for byte-identical review material (`ouroboros/tools/commit_gate.py`): the gate refuses an identical staged diff for free from the first verdict block (`identical_diff_refused`); `review_change` reuses the settled record of an identical request at $0 on every subject. A rebuttal is identified by its content sha256: a hash new to the streak buys exactly one paid re-review; a repeated hash is refused free. The two axes stay distinct: refusal-streak eligibility is about verdicts (a rebuttal is spent only by the substantive verdict it bought); money is about dispatch (every dispatched wave counts whatever its terminal; an assembly refusal dispatched nothing and stays outside the count). Exhaustion is the typed `review_cycles_exhausted` event and starts no automatic commit: under Advisory (Cyber Pro included) the author decides whether what was reviewed is enough (finish, or land stating what was not); Blocking keeps the ceiling menu and the corrected work and stops the action. `review_change` answers alike, result waves included. An author continuation is distinct from a paid `review_rebuttal`; a stop never creates PASS or new review budget. Enforced by `tests/test_review_cycles.py`.

The review-contract fingerprint covers the reviewer roster, routes, enforcement, resolved efforts, writable session rights, each seat's asked parts and the text Ouroboros writes into a seat's request (frames, sections, rules, pulse question, answer contracts, schemas, navigation, native replies' reading guidance, session, checking-copy and native wrappers), rendered by its owners. Request data, tool results, text shared with other runs and guides (`BIBLE.md`, `docs/CHECKLISTS.md`, both books, `docs/DESIGN.md`) stay out of it and every reuse key; records keep their rules. Trade-off: old verdicts can replay under amended guides. Enforced by `tests/test_review_reuse_identity.py`, `tests/test_review_contract_completeness.py`.

### External PR review is not commit authorization

The authoring agent freezes the final committed base-to-head range and hands it to a separate agent context for read-only review; same-conversation self-review does not count; an unavailable review is recorded `NOT_RUN`, never presented as clean. `CONTRIBUTING.md` owns the public procedure and evidence fields. `scripts/run_external_review.py` has two lanes. Without `--contributor` it runs the commit gate's own non-committing cycle over the staged index in the runtime's isolated checkout of that patch; `--preflight-reviewer` adds the named look, whose record and full answer land in `preflight.json` / `preflight.txt`. With `--contributor` it runs the runtime `review_change` operation over a committed `base..head` proposal: maintainer tooling that freezes the pool rows, binds each record seat to its prompt and response receipts and records exact base/head/tree/diff hashes, route facts, settlement and redacted transcripts; a missing, tampered, drifted or contradictory receipt, or a record naming another subject, makes a passing run `INCOMPLETE`; a blocked run keeps its cause beside the mismatches. Either lane executes the installed body's review flow and rules — the clean checkout the wrapper runs from, for a contributor the target base — never the proposal's copy; a checkout that already contains the proposal is refused before anything is spent. A seat still open leaves the record `pending`, reported as `review_custody_unresolved`, never as a verdict; the operator lane then keeps its checkout.

The operation runs no tests. The contributor lane first runs the proposal's hermetic suite with the commit gate's runner in an isolated checkout of the same frozen subject: a failure is the typed `tests_preflight_blocked` refusal before any reviewer is paid; a pass reaches every seat before dispatch as the frozen `preflight_execution` exhibit (tested on its base) and attaches to that tree's review record (`review_ledger.attach_tests_evidence`, `proof: candidate_bound`); any other tree leaves `NOT_RUN`. The contributor lane drives its own data root before any config load: host settings are read in place under the integrity pin, their panel outranking an inherited env; `--run-cap-usd` is the isolated ledger's whole global limit, never the saved `TOTAL_BUDGET` nor the installation's concurrent budget authority; Claudexor rows run only through `--attach-host-engine`, attach-only. The packet is readiness evidence, never commit, push, merge or publication authority: maintainers choose the landing parent and release version, preserve authorship and run the final exact-candidate gate. Enforced by `tests/test_external_review_script.py` and `tests/test_contributor_review_isolation.py`.

### Release sync

A pull request into `ouroboros` keeps version carriers byte-identical to its target (`release_sync.VERSION_CARRIER_SPANS`; ARCHITECTURE §10, invariant 2). Ordinary serving commits, including documentation, require a numbered release; prepared body candidates may be neutral. Committing paths use candidate/update authority; review-only paths admit neutral contributions. Managed integration keeps the official version. `preflight_review(deterministic_only=True, source="worktree" | "index")` gives free release diagnostics and worktree book balance, no freshness. Separate source failures from findings; check staging and unchanged files/index/state (`tests/test_release_metadata_diagnostics.py`; contracts: ARCHITECTURE §6 "Commit preflight"). At integration `release_sync.sync_release_metadata()` projects the chosen version; `release_metadata_findings()` checks carriers, the current README row and the P9 limits, without autofix; changelog prose and trimming stay deliberate (a milestone's row opens `**Short title** — **type: heading.**`). Packaging: ARCHITECTURE §8 "Build scripts".

Hermetic preflight isolation: §14 "The tiered commit and CI gates". A test rebinds every imported process-global root and fails closed on the live data root; setting `OUROBOROS_DATA_DIR` alone is not isolation. Enforced by `tests/conftest.py`. A reviewed local commit is the durability boundary of self-modification; an `origin` push and CI are follow-up signals, not prerequisites for its survival (ARCHITECTURE §8).
