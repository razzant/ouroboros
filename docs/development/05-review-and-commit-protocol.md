# Review & Commit Protocol

This chapter owns the rules a reviewed commit obeys across its three stages — prepared preflight, the authoritative gate and publication binding — plus the shared paid-cycle cap, free replay, external-review evidence and the release-sync rules a pull request must obey. Its one shaping decision: technical failure and commit permission are separate facts, so no rule here lets a missing review become a PASS. `docs/CHECKLISTS.md` owns reviewer questions, severity and output contracts; ARCHITECTURE §6 "Git and commit review" and "Review stack" own the mechanism.

Finish the edits and focused tests, then call `commit_reviewed`; `preflight_reviewer` (or standalone `preflight_review(reviewer=…)`) buys one named catalog row's earlier look. `commit_reviewed` lands only in the system repository, and any other `root` is a typed `TOOL_ARG_ERROR`; a project copy or another registered root is reviewed with `review_change` — the same wave and one ledger record under that root's checklist layer, nothing committed — and committed there with ordinary git (ARCHITECTURE §6 "Change review on any root"). Enforced by `tests/test_commit_gate.py` and `tests/test_review_change_end_to_end.py`.

1. **Prepared preflight.** Authorization and unresolved-work checks come before file preparation, staging and the fingerprint; free-cycle and budget admission come before any paid look. The free deterministic checks and the independently applicable tests preflight run on every commit; a named row then looks once, inline with the full `review_rebuttal`, and only informs. `skip_advisory_review=True` records `preflight: skipped` and no name records `not_performed`: facts, not audited bypasses. A free replay buys no wave, and an explicit test skip is not green proof (ARCHITECTURE §6 "Commit preflight").
2. **Review and author response.** A clean supported review keeps its one-call path. A material ordinary Advisory outcome returns before any Git effect; Main may finish unchanged, correct, request another permitted panel or stop. Free continuation passes the returned `review_reference` with `author_disposition` and rationale, binds the current attributed bytes and reruns the independent required checks without another critic. Blocking needs fresh reviewer approval (ARCHITECTURE §6 "Git and commit review").
3. **Publication binding.** The created commit or tag is checked against that same binding before push; a mutation, rebase, conflict resolution or changed landing parent needs a fresh current-candidate binding under the applicable critic or Advisory author authority, with the same independent checks.

Keep optional task evidence after the stable governance prefix and shrink it before any existing review material; a source pointer gives a packet-only row no retrieval; removing an ignored view never deletes the canonical source (ARCHITECTURE §6 "Commit review evidence").

### Ledger honesty and failure

Each wave or free pre-dispatch refusal leaves an `ouroboros/review_ledger.py` record, linked to outcome and attempt by `review_record_id` (ARCHITECTURE §6 "Review ledger record"). Keep no `PASS` without a usable answer; $0 refusals `NOT_DISPATCHED`; open custody `pending`; same-attempt settlement raises `revision`; unobserved models `unknown`. The record informs, never authorizes. Keep reported zero/partial and absent cost separate from status and billing finality. Checkpoint failures retain primary acquisition and cleanup causes as local authority errors in the existing diagnostic carrier. Tests: `tests/test_review_ledger.py`, `tests/test_review_cost_knowledge.py`, `tests/test_review_checkpoint_cause.py`.

A technical review failure keeps its phase, reason, received findings and full result recorded as failure, never PASS. Once its typed outcome reaches Main it may support explicit Advisory author continuation on a known, independently bound candidate; outside Cyber Pro, Blocking still blocks. Cyber may continue without a new review, keeping the original findings and pending invocation; it never fabricates candidate bytes, custody or physical effects, and Stop, deadline and cost facts stay recorded.

A named look is its own `review_change` wave: it holds no commit custody and never answers the panel. Pending wave reconciliation keeps the prepared index and never restages a lost one (ARCHITECTURE §6 "Late completion and typed refusals"). Hosted-review identity is mechanism (ARCHITECTURE §6 "Session identity"); a change keeps the full raw result, ordinary PASS rows and genuine empty-clean responses in tests, and keeps the contributor checker refusing unconfirmed model identity, display labels included.

### Review pool and paid cycles

The review pool is the enabled `review_eligible` rows of `OUROBOROS_SUBAGENTS`; effort and delivery are row properties, duplicate model ids stay independent seats, and `config.adaptive_quorum` owns quorum. Outside Cyber Pro every pool row sits; Cyber Pro composes the panel from the pool and records the reason. Every seat receives one brief — the change question, plus on every retrieving seat the coupling question across the whole repository, in every context mode; there is no separate scope role (ARCHITECTURE §6 "The coupling question (Part 2 of the brief)"). Window size neither grants nor removes authority. Reading coverage is diagnostic on every route: keep it beside the verdict, and never let it exclude a responding reviewer from quorum, block a commit or buy another review — the author judges whether a concrete gap needs more reading. Failed delivery or brief assembly is never a clean verdict. A retrieving delivery canonicalizes by output shape (`triad_review.review_output_shape`: `array` | `object` | `report` | `two_part`), never by surface name; a new surface registers in the shape table.

Paid review cycles across every gate share one owner knob, `OUROBOROS_REVIEW_MAX_CYCLES` — a string: a positive integer or `unlimited`; a malformed value fails closed to the shipped default. Its SSOT is `ouroboros/review_cycles.py`; the four per-gate meanings live in ARCHITECTURE §6 "Review stack", the default in §7 "Default settings". `unlimited` removes only the local count; deadline, budget and lifecycle rails still bind. The paid fact is stamped write-ahead at physical dispatch, so a pre-transport refusal — panel assembly, an unavailable route, a wallet veto — consumes nothing and stays retryable (ARCHITECTURE §6 "Paid stamp and owner custody").

Never pay for byte-identical review material (`ouroboros/tools/commit_gate.py`): the gate refuses an identical staged diff for free from the first verdict block (`identical_diff_refused`), and skill review replays a recorded substantive verdict for an identical snapshot at $0 while the persisted state still covers it. A rebuttal is identified by its content sha256: a hash new to the streak buys exactly one paid re-review; a repeated hash is refused free. The two axes stay distinct: refusal-streak eligibility is about verdicts (a rebuttal is spent only by the substantive verdict it bought); money is about dispatch (every dispatched wave counts whatever its terminal; an assembly refusal dispatched nothing and stays outside the count). A refusal that spent nothing is a typed `not_dispatched` fact, never a verdict. Exhaustion is the typed `review_cycles_exhausted` event and starts no automatic commit: Advisory may continue explicitly from the received reference with a loud author decision; Blocking keeps the corrected work and stops without authorizing the action. An author continuation is distinct from a paid `review_rebuttal`, and a stop never creates PASS or new review budget. Enforced by `tests/test_review_cycles.py`.

The review-contract fingerprint covers the reviewer roster, routes, enforcement, resolved efforts, the review prompt constants, each seat's asked parts, both answer contracts and the required-source policy (the session serialization only when Skill Review holds an agent-session row) and deliberately excludes the contents of `BIBLE.md`, `docs/CHECKLISTS.md`, `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`, this handbook and `docs/DESIGN.md`, so editing those documents neither lapses recorded verdicts nor frees replays. The accepted trade-off: an old verdict can replay under amended governance text, rather than routine documentation maintenance repricing every recorded review.

### External PR review is not commit authorization

The authoring agent freezes the final committed base-to-head range and hands it to a separate agent context for read-only review; same-conversation self-review does not count, and an unavailable review is recorded `NOT_RUN`, never presented as clean. `CONTRIBUTING.md` owns the public procedure and evidence fields. `scripts/run_external_review.py` has two lanes. Without `--contributor` it runs the commit gate's own non-committing cycle over the staged index in the runtime's isolated checkout of the staged patch; `--preflight-reviewer` adds the named look, whose record and full answer land in `preflight.json` / `preflight.txt`. With `--contributor` it runs the runtime `review_change` operation over a committed `base..head` proposal: maintainer tooling that freezes the pool rows, binds every seat of the review record to its prompt and response receipts, and records exact base/head/tree/diff hashes, route facts, settlement and redacted transcripts; a missing, tampered, drifted or contradictory receipt, or a record naming another subject, makes the packet `INCOMPLETE`. Either lane executes the installed body's review flow and rules — the clean checkout the wrapper runs from, for a contributor the target base — never the proposal's copy; a checkout that already contains the proposal is refused before anything is spent. A seat still open leaves the record `pending`, reported as `review_custody_unresolved`, never as a verdict; the operator lane then keeps its checkout.

The operation runs no tests. The contributor lane first runs the proposal's hermetic suite with the commit gate's runner in an isolated checkout of the same frozen subject: a failure is the typed `tests_preflight_blocked` refusal before any reviewer is paid; a pass attaches to the review record of that same tree (`review_ledger.attach_tests_evidence`, `proof: candidate_bound`), and any other tree leaves `NOT_RUN`. The contributor lane drives its own data root before any config load: host settings are read in place under the integrity pin, their panel outranking an inherited env; `--run-cap-usd` is the isolated ledger's whole global limit, never the saved `TOTAL_BUDGET` nor the installation's concurrent budget authority; Claudexor rows run only through `--attach-host-engine`, attach-only. The packet is readiness evidence, never commit, push, merge or publication authority: maintainers choose the landing parent and release version, preserve authorship and run the final exact-candidate gate. Enforced by `tests/test_external_review_script.py` and `tests/test_contributor_review_isolation.py`.

### Release sync

A pull request into `ouroboros` leaves every version carrier byte-identical to its target (the carrier list and the one projection that writes them: ARCHITECTURE §10, invariant 2). Use `preflight_review(commit_message="...", deterministic_only=True, source="worktree" | "index")` for free release diagnostics and a separately labelled worktree book balance before review spend; it grants no freshness. Keep source failures separate from candidate findings, and verify partial staging with unchanged files, index and state (`tests/test_release_metadata_diagnostics.py`; contracts: ARCHITECTURE §6 "Commit preflight"). At integration `release_sync.sync_release_metadata()` projects the chosen version; `release_metadata_findings()` checks carriers, the current README row and the P9 limits; changelog prose and trimming stay deliberate, and diagnostics add no autofix. Packaging: ARCHITECTURE §8 "Build scripts".

The hermetic preflight runs in a disposable worktree with temporary data, settings and pycache and a scrubbed runtime/secret-class environment (ARCHITECTURE §6 "Hermetic preflight proof"). A test rebinds every imported process-global root and fails closed on the live data root; setting `OUROBOROS_DATA_DIR` alone is not isolation. Enforced by `tests/conftest.py`. A reviewed local commit is the durability boundary of self-modification; an `origin` push and CI are follow-up signals, not prerequisites for its survival (ARCHITECTURE §8).
