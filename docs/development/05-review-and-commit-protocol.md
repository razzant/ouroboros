# Review & Commit Protocol

This chapter owns preflight, review, publication binding, paid-cycle reuse and release sync. Technical failure and commit permission are separate facts: missing review never becomes PASS. `docs/CHECKLISTS.md` owns reviewer questions, severity and output contracts; ARCHITECTURE §6 "Git and commit review" and "Review stack" own the mechanism.

After edits and focused tests, call `commit_reviewed` (system repo only; any other `root` is a typed `TOOL_ARG_ERROR`); review other registered roots with `review_change` and commit them with ordinary git. Optional `preflight_reviewer` (or standalone `preflight_review(reviewer=…)`) buys one named catalog row's early look (ARCHITECTURE §6 "Change review on any root"; `tests/test_commit_gate.py`, `tests/test_review_change_end_to_end.py`).

1. **Prepared preflight.** Authorization and unresolved-work checks precede preparation, staging and fingerprinting; free-cycle and budget admission precede paid looks. Deterministic checks and tests preflight run on every commit. A named look advises on the full `review_rebuttal`; never buy it with a new panel's last wave. `skip_advisory_review=True` records `preflight: skipped`; when `preflight_reviewer` is omitted and no skip is set, the preflight status is `not_performed`. No wave left for the look also records `not_performed`, with its reason. Free replay buys no wave; skipping tests proves no pass (ARCHITECTURE §6 "Commit preflight").
2. **Review and author response.** Every outcome (PASS or pending) returns before Git so Main may finish unchanged, correct, request another panel or stop. Free continuation passes `review_reference` with `author_disposition` and rationale (optional `subagent_check` records `review_replaced_by_subagents`, never a seat or PASS), rebinds current bytes and reruns required checks without another critic.
3. **Publication binding.** Commits and tags verify that same binding before push; any mutation, rebase, conflict resolution or changed landing parent requires a fresh current-candidate binding under the applicable critic or the author's authority, with the same checks.

Place optional task evidence after the stable governance prefix and shrink it before existing review material; a source pointer grants packet-only rows no retrieval, and removing an ignored view never deletes the canonical source (ARCHITECTURE §6 "Commit review evidence").

### Ledger honesty and failure

Every wave or free pre-dispatch refusal writes an `ouroboros/review_ledger.py` record linked to outcome and attempt by `review_record_id` (ARCHITECTURE §6 "Review ledger record"). Never record `PASS` without a usable answer: $0 refusals are `NOT_DISPATCHED`, open custody `pending`, same-attempt settlement raises `revision`, unobserved models stay `unknown`. Records inform and never authorize; keep reported zero/partial/absent cost separate from status and billing finality, and retain primary checkpoint acquisition and cleanup causes as local authority errors in the existing diagnostic carrier. Tests: `tests/test_review_ledger.py`, `tests/test_review_cost_knowledge.py`, `tests/test_review_checkpoint_cause.py`.

Technical review failures keep phase, reason, received findings and full result as failure, never PASS. Once delivered to Main, their typed outcome may support explicit author continuation on a known, independently bound candidate; Cyber may continue without a new review while preserving original findings, pending invocations, Stop, deadline and cost facts — never fabricating candidate bytes, custody or effects.

Named `review_change` owns no commit custody or panel verdict. Prepared/lost reconciliation preserves the index without restaging; same-candidate/intent retries collect original paid seats, never replacements (ARCHITECTURE §6 "Late completion and typed refusals"). Keep full raw, PASS and empty-clean results; contributor checks require exact IDs or bound catalog proof (§6 "Session identity"), never labels alone; other refusals keep their causes.

### Review pool and paid cycles

The review pool is the enabled `review_eligible` rows of `OUROBOROS_SUBAGENTS`: effort and delivery are row properties, duplicate model IDs stay independent seats, and `config.adaptive_quorum` owns quorum. Outside Cyber Pro named pool rows sit (narrowing requires a reason); Cyber Pro composes freely, and unmarked additions advise without counting. Each seat gets one brief — the change question plus, on retrieving seats, the repo-wide coupling question (no separate scope role; ARCHITECTURE §6 "The coupling question (Part 2 of the brief)"). Window size never changes authority, and reading coverage is diagnostic on every route: keep it beside the verdict, never exclude a responding seat from quorum, block a commit or buy another review; the author judges whether a concrete gap needs more reading. Failed delivery/assembly is never clean. Retrieving deliveries canonicalize by output shape (`triad_review.review_output_shape`: `array` | `object` | `report` | `two_part` | `plan_reply`), never surface name; register new surfaces there.

`OUROBOROS_REVIEW_MAX_CYCLES` bounds three paid pools, excluding plan asks (`ouroboros/review_cycles.py`; value and pools: ARCHITECTURE §6 "Review stack"; default: §7 "Default settings"). `unlimited` removes only the count cap; deadline, budget and lifecycle rails still bind. Stamp paid waves at physical dispatch: refusals before transport (assembly, route, money, Pause), replays and collections consume no cycle (ARCHITECTURE §6 "Paid stamp and owner custody").

Never pay twice for identical material (`ouroboros/tools/commit_gate.py`): settled usable verdicts and identical `review_change` requests collect at $0; answered, settled Cyber Pro adviser-only waves reuse as advice at $0 without counting as verdicts; waves without usable results pay again, and a `review_rebuttal` SHA joins the identity, so a new one buys a new wave. `review_cycles_exhausted` starts no commit — the author decides whether to finish or land disclosing the gap (result waves follow the same rule). Continuation is distinct from rebuttal; stopping creates neither PASS nor budget (`tests/test_review_cycles.py`).

The review-contract fingerprint covers roster, routes, enforcement, resolved efforts, writable session rights, each seat's asked parts and all text Ouroboros writes into a seat's request (frames, sections, rules, pulse question, answer contracts, schemas, navigation, native replies' reading guidance, session, checking-copy and native wrappers) rendered by its owners — excluding request data, tool results, shared run text and guides (`BIBLE.md`, `docs/CHECKLISTS.md`, both books, `docs/DESIGN.md`) from it and every reuse key; records keep their rules (trade-off: old verdicts can replay under amended guides; `tests/test_review_reuse_identity.py`, `tests/test_review_contract_completeness.py`).

### External PR review is not commit authorization

Freeze the final committed `base..head` range and hand it to an independent reviewer who leaves the candidate untouched (writable sessions use private checking copies); same-conversation self-review never counts, and unavailable review is `NOT_RUN`, never clean (procedure and packet fields: `CONTRIBUTING.md`). `scripts/run_external_review.py` runs the installed body's review flow and rules, never the proposal's, from its clean checkout (for a contributor, the target base), refusing any checkout containing the proposal before spend:

- **Operator lane** (default): the non-committing commit-gate cycle over an isolated staged patch; optional `--preflight-reviewer` writes `preflight.json`/`preflight.txt`.
- **`--contributor` lane** (maintainer tooling): `review_change` over committed `base..head`, freezing pool rows, binding each record seat to its prompt and response receipts, and recording exact base/head/tree/diff hashes, route facts, settlement and redacted transcripts; missing, tampered, drifted or contradictory receipts, or a record naming another subject, make a passing run `INCOMPLETE` (blocked runs keep their cause). `review_change` runs no tests, so the lane first runs the proposal's hermetic suite with the commit gate's runner in an isolated checkout of the frozen subject: failure refuses `tests_preflight_blocked` at $0; a pass reaches every seat before dispatch as the frozen `preflight_execution` exhibit (tested on its base) and attaches to that tree's review record via `review_ledger.attach_tests_evidence` (`proof: candidate_bound`; other trees stay `NOT_RUN`). It uses its own data root before config load, reads host settings under an integrity pin (panel outranks env), treats `--run-cap-usd` as its whole isolated limit (never `TOTAL_BUDGET` or live budget authority), and reaches Claudexor only via attach-only `--attach-host-engine`.

Open seats stay `pending` / `review_custody_unresolved`, never a verdict; the operator lane then keeps its checkout. The packet is readiness evidence, never commit, push, merge or publication authority: maintainers choose the landing parent, preserve authorship and run the final exact-candidate gate (`tests/test_external_review_script.py`, `tests/test_contributor_review_isolation.py`).

### Release sync

PRs into `ouroboros` keep version carriers byte-identical to target (`release_sync.VERSION_CARRIER_SPANS`; ARCHITECTURE §10, invariant 2). Separate source failures from findings and check staging plus unchanged files/index/state (`tests/test_release_metadata_diagnostics.py`; ARCHITECTURE §6 "Commit preflight"). At integration `release_sync.sync_release_metadata()` projects the chosen version; `release_metadata_findings()` checks carriers, README row and P9 limits without autofix; changelog prose and trimming stay deliberate (milestone row opens `**Short title** — **type: heading.**`; packaging: ARCHITECTURE §8 "Build scripts").

Maintainer cycle between releases, marker to promotion (BIBLE P9): CONTRIBUTING "Maintainer Boundary"; `tests/test_development_cycle.py`.

Hermetic preflight isolation (DEVELOPMENT §14 "The tiered commit and CI gates"): tests rebind every imported process-global root and fail closed on the live data root (`OUROBOROS_DATA_DIR` alone is not isolation; `tests/conftest.py`). A reviewed local commit is the durability boundary of self-modification; `origin` push and CI are follow-up signals, not survival prerequisites (ARCHITECTURE §8).
