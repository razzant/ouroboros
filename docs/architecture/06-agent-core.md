# 6. Agent Core

`loop.py` runs tasks, direct turns and wakes; admission, completion, satisfaction, verification, review and artifact finality stay separate facts, never converted into one another.

### Task lifecycle

`OuroborosAgent` runs pooled tasks and the mind's foci in workers (§5 Conversation lane). `agent_task_pipeline.py` freezes contract/context, runs the loop, stores results/artifacts and root post-work (`post_task_synthesis.py`, `post_task_checkpoint.py`), then publishes. Store rows, admitted by `task_result_schema.py` (§11), govern lifecycle (`get_task_result`, `query_data`); result JSON is import/export; usage owns money/tokens. Crash: `failure.kind="runtime"`; lost capture stays unknown. `finalization_timing.py` times delivery; final evidence reads fresh. `supervisor/cognitive_operations.py` keeps typed operations out of idle inference; deadlines/budgets/cancellation stay.

`task_store` reads the stable terminal page plus uncertain rows; the gateway applies effective global top N. `task_status` derives stability per body write; `store_hot_facts` backfills legacy NULLs by CAS; GET never writes.

The transcript is append-only for byte-prefix caching (§6 Prompt caching): `transcript_prefix.py` counts breaks by origin, blocking no send, and before pricing retires signed reasoning from the first change, or all of it on system/tools changes or without a baseline. `model_execution` records requested/routed/reported models of the last accepted ordinary solve; empty/forced/review/post-task replies never replace it. `loop_nudges.py` asks the successor once after tool use; real work or an accepted text/hash settles it (DESIGN §5; CLIENT_CONTRACT §6).

`loop_delivery.py` keeps exact `DeliveryCandidate` bytes through verification/review failures. `outcomes.py` separates execution/objective/review/artifacts/child-absorption/custody: receipts/exact references prove effects, tool errors alone never degrade answers, unevaluated objectives keep `residual_tool_errors_without_review`, `completed` can be unaccepted. `FINAL ANSWER:` always survives; only `task_contract.answer_protocol="final_answer_line"` prompts it. Armed/sticky selection rejects direct array-wrapped controls as malformed; ordinary JSON is unchanged. Reasons persist; owner wording projects on read (§3).

Answers carry `terminal_host_notice` (plan/orphan limits) and, for nonempty custody, the owner's `terminal_custody_notice`; notices keep answer bytes/verdict and revive no approval; child/plan evidence hash them, and changes void dispositions. Presence omits host status. Stop/reap salvage is a bounded task-wide preview, never a final answer (full text: `get_task_result`/stop receipts); the reader labels cancelled rows `[CANCELLED_BY]`, others `[OWNER_CONTROL] command=stop_now|wrap_up|unknown` by recorded policy.

Prose outside selection episodes finishes unless recovery is needed. Finish checks the task's own result left unexamined since it last changed (a typed no-send review examined nothing), a failed last check, running children/open runs, reviews, skills/handover, owner sources, material, services and admission (`completion_held`); facts and shown nonblocking reviews leave prose fresh (`delivery_facts.py`, `displaced_answer`, silence), other debts need `finish_task(action=finish|stop, answer|answer_sha256)`. Conflicting live/restored batches hold; compaction withdraws unavailable selectors. Stop delivers selected unfinished work without waits, review, generation, descendant cancellation or approval; no evidence undoes it. Presence counts a send only on host-confirmed delivery.

Provider death salvages text but ends `failed`/`infra_failed` with `provider_unavailable` (or `resource_refusal_no_resend`) and an immediate not-completed owner notice; `terminal_provider_notice` quotes the provider, never advising blind replay. One forced model call runs only while a call can land; wait expiry and `loop_llm_call.provider_no_call_source` forbid another paid window. The finalization sink stamps `model_final`, `host_notice` or `host_salvage`; a forced reply requesting tools beyond one `finish_task` is incomplete, a whole one survives a disclosed length cut, and its verdict rides `trace_refs`; finalization controls are typed mailbox entries. Descendant activity is not the task's own progress; an unkillable process stays visibly running; no final cost, even zero, settles `delegated_runs_unreconciled`.

#### Result review

`review_change(subject="result")` (`tools/review_result.py`) asks the pool whether the result, and any method the owner fixed, does what was asked (CHECKLISTS `Result Review Checklist`, `result_rubric`). Subject: what was asked (request fields, `effective_acceptance_claims` (§11), owner's words), the reply, what the task produced and host-attested verification receipts, never the work trace. `result_material`: registered artifacts (empty, MCP read blobs too), changes against the admitted baseline and its `attributed_commit` epochs, paths of observed native writes (same-byte: `completed_no_effect`) and `observed_effect` receipts (failed calls' too), as exact copies and excerpts; a failed capture is a gap, not absence. Inputs and expected outputs are intent. Text is retained once (`result-subject-<sha16>`); its sha, the wave identity, reuses the settled record at $0.

`review_change` owns the wave: panel (`compose_panel`), paid stamp, custody, rejoin and settlement (`surface="result"`); addressed mail or Pause leaves it with `review_pause`, owing a notice or late mark. Seats answer `PART_RESULT` without coupling, under adaptive quorum; failed rows are infrastructure evidence, pre-send refusal `not_dispatched`. Children may ask.

Modes (`OUROBOROS_TASK_REVIEW_MODE`, §7). Auto forces nothing; Off refuses `subject=result` before payment. Required reviews root deliveries with produced work (observed production, a `review_answer` contract, an adapter answer line): absent a settled subject-bound record, the host collects a running wave at $0 or seats the pool (`chosen_by=host`). An outcome not received whole, PASS included, is handed back once; then PASS delivers and anything else delivers on the author's next finish (accepting that subject). After a received review a revision is the author's (`_revised`, Presence's too): its reasoned finish accepts it at once; an earlier PASS stays that version's. The running review is awaited unless `pending_review=finish` gives a rationale after answers ready since. A spent allowance keeps one $0 refusal per subject/ceiling; a review that missed newer owner words shows them and an unread outcome once; shown facts survive same-ID restore. Presence speech is never held; its review owes late feedback. `review_decision` says why none ran. A send receipt names reviews of its bytes in this task's recent records, or `unknown` if inconclusive (`owner_delivery.show_record`), never a gate.

Objective authority. Every mode but Off binds the settled review of the delivered result into the task's review runs (`authority="host_root"`, `binding_hash` = subject sha); a new candidate or re-nominated material (any rail, same words too) supersedes earlier runs without deleting them; an author acceptance (`informed_finish`) counts only on a current run of its subject. `_objective_axis` takes the reviewers' PASS/FAIL (a blocked tier fails), or PASS from `author_acceptance` (`author_finish`, `author_finish_pending`, `previous_revision_accepted`) shown as Done with warnings unless beside a PASS; a result run stored under Blocking enforcement whose cap bought no review reads as blocked FAIL (`review_cycles_exhausted`). A late PASS of a pending finish's exact subject clears only that warning (`late_review_marks`). A result PASS never satisfies the commit gate.

Allowance and price. At `OUROBOROS_REVIEW_MAX_CYCLES` the author decides on reviewed work; the owner's ceiling holds. `review_price.py` reports median, count and range of this row's fully priced waves on its current model, never estimates or partial bills; with none, `cost unknown`; a session `uses a session seat and time`, a local route `no API cost per review`. `## Review seat activity`, `GET /api/review-pool` (`basis: observed_reviews`), Settings and onboarding share that price; until reviews are measured Settings can show the model's published token tariff, a rate rather than a cost per review.

Root admission seal (`delivery_seal.py`, re-exported from `loop`). Only at an author stop, right before delivery (`loop_delivery._seal_admission_before_delivery`), sealing closes the root's admission (no new subtask under it) and catches a late owner message by generation (docstring); a plain finish never opens it, so only the local admission-lock drain catches an owner message routed at the supervisor. Persisted names keep the task-acceptance wording (docstring). The supervisor owns the row under Q (`queue_transitions.transition_acceptance_fence`: `end` compares `owner_message_generation`, then seals or releases) and clears it at `task_done`; a pooled fence binds its attempt and PID; if a begin goes unanswered, the worker queues release of its own token after the sends (`agent.py`); a proven-ended attempt's active fence is released at the handoff to its successor, and a consumed exact Resume rebinds a park fence. `ok` seals; only owner facts revise: a generation mismatch or pending owner mail makes `_release_for_owner_followup` pop `task_completion` and end the fence with `revision` (`_owner_generation_changed`). `sealed` on begin is the worker's own lost ack; `stale_attempt` means lost queue ownership and no publication; any other refusal, `unknown` included, is a gap, never a refusal of the answer (§10 invariant 25).

Owner-source acknowledgement (`finish_task` argument `acceptance_subject`). While control is armed or facts left a fresh answer, `loop_messages.prepare_acceptance_observation` appends an `[ACCEPTANCE_SUBJECT_OBSERVATION]` row when owner-source facts change (earlier rows stay). `acknowledge_acceptance_observation` advances only consumed ingress, never criteria or a verdict, and refuses with a typed `ACK_*` cause for an unobserved source, unread input or a changed source or generation (`acceptance_source_ack`, §10 invariant 21). Raw owner messages stay evidence; their count changes no requirement.
