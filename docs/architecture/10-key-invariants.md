# 10. Key Invariants

This part gives me the rules every other part keeps: cross-cutting owners and the continuity map (exact sources, working views and authority), and the closed roads (§10.2), approaches already rejected and why. A review of a change to my body carries it whole; before non-trivial work on my body I consult it with the owning chapter (BIBLE P6).

1. **Constitution and identity persist.** Outside Cyber Pro neither `BIBLE.md` nor `identity.md` is deleted; content evolves.
2. **Release metadata has one projection.** `VERSION` is canonical; `ouroboros/tools/release_sync.py::VERSION_CARRIER_SPANS` names carriers, `sync_release_metadata()` writes them, `version_carrier_desyncs()` checks them, and BIBLE P9 governs neutral commits.
3. **Configuration and messaging have single owners.** `ouroboros/config.py` is the facade while leaves (`settings_defaults`, `settings_scales`, `model_slots`, `review_model_routes`, `runtime_limits`, `settings_integrity`) own keys, defaults and verified reads (§7). Messages traverse `supervisor/message_bus.py`; task transitions are `ouroboros/store.py` transactions (`store_switch.py` owns boot import and restart exit); other stores use their file lock.
4. **The usage store is the record of spend.** `state/usage.sqlite` (`ouroboros/usage_store.py`) owns attempts and summaries; state, task, event and UI amounts are attempt-keyed projections.
5. **The app is a replaceable host.** Packaged installs verify `repo.bundle` and manifest once to run the managed checkout (§2, §8); Restart keeps its tip, updates apply approved exact SHAs only, host facts re-derive each launch, owners repoint kept host paths (`config.py`), and no tracked name is app-typed.
6. **Shutdown stops what it owns, once, within the grace** (§9). Close, Restart and Fault stop `state/owned_processes.json` executors and task processes while sparing the daemon, which ends only on Quit, non-downgrade pin change, Panic or Restart Claudexor (§3) by measured ledger identity. First door starts grace and stamps stops; signals need measured identity, next boot retries obligations before admission, no agent delays Panic, and clients never queue or replay it (`owned_shutdown.py`).
7. **This book is the present-tense map.** Structural changes update their owning chapter in the same commit (DEVELOPMENT "Documentation contract").
8. **Skill gates do not collapse.** Discovery, deterministic preflight, digest-bound critic/qualified author authority, owner grants, dependency readiness, enablement and execution stay separate (§13): neither verdict nor `enabled=true` proves readiness.
9. **Startup rescue has one mutation owner.** Supervisor recovery records rescue evidence before reset or blocks preserving the tree (§2); workers/agents only warn without staging or committing dirt, and stale `index.lock` files never age out (refused checkouts fail).
10. **Current facts are stored, not re-derived.** Owning transitions publish addressed facts and obligations in their store transaction; readers capture coherent reads, then materialize off-transaction. History serves cognition, audits and explicit repair, never ordinary authority or answering paths. One `current_stop` per scope reads installation → tree → member (release removes it; `_resume_selection` keeps choice identities, not cause); technical refusal, execution evidence and permission stay separate, uncertainty visibly holds only dependent effects even on terminals, observations close once no live producer remains, and outbox history follows commit (`store.py`, `usage_store.py`, `store_switch.py`, `obligations.py`, `work_stop.py`, `owned_shutdown.py`).
11. **UI resources carry a disposer.** Subscriptions, listeners, observers, timers, streams and live pages tear down explicitly so navigation leaves no hidden mutator (§3).
12. **Frozen contracts extend explicitly.** `ouroboros/contracts/` is a versioned backward-compatible ABI (§11); new capabilities extend shapes or ship versioned successors without breaking consumers.
13. **Provider wire adaptation stays exact-route and success-confirmed.** Physical projections adjust values, fields or dialect on one provider/endpoint/API/model only; failed candidates teach nothing durable, local degradation never governs dispatch, and `state/usage.sqlite` stays distinct from `usage.request_wire`.
14. **Cancellation is intent-then-custody.** Custody mutation needs durable watchdog-replayable intent and exclusive claim; settled results prove no dead worker (live ownership gates cancel), and natural completion wins keeping its stored row byte-identical (§5). Cascades follow lineage roots, owed delivery precedes intent settlement, finals list task-addressed open runs, closing receipts and pending non-review invocations, live enqueue precedes annotation, and resource release is synchronous (`supervisor/task_lifecycle.py`, `ouroboros/cancel_intents.py`, `supervisor/cancel_publication.py`, `supervisor/terminal_delivery.py`, `ouroboros/delegate_terminal.py`).
15. **Cancellation registry corruption stays visible.** In `state/cancel_intents.json` and `state/terminal_deliveries.json`, absence reads empty while malformed files/rows disclose with bytes kept, never rebuilt from `{}` (which would drop cancels or delivery debts).
16. **Verification receipts reconcile by one typed identity key.** Sameness requires equal `(kind, value)` keys without cross-kind matching, failing safe toward fewer reconciliations while disclosed parts never compare (`_outcome_receipts.py`).
17. **Review spend has one ceiling.** Paid review gates share `OUROBOROS_REVIEW_MAX_CYCLES` (`review_cycles.py`, §6; plan asks excluded); the ceiling keeps the final author reaction, and blocking corrections or stops grant no approval.
18. **Admission and release share locks.** Confirm the id; preparations (reviews too) and pending starts hold it. Live, reserved, unknown or technically continuable work holds non-review registrations (author stop adds no hold). Workspace use holds without owning until engine `candidateMatch` rules the folder out (ended runs through Keep/Continue by owed duties; unknown holds); release requires original creation, exact receipt store/id/root and clear Keep gates, holding id and folder while unanswered (§6; `delegate_registration_policy`, `delegate_workspace_hold`).
19. **An interrupted parent leaves no orphan.** Admitted children inherit parent cancellation across teardown and restore (`kill_workers`, `pending_parent_interrupted`); preparation-only requests carry durable tokens for typed `recover_interrupted_preparations` because snapshot absence proves no non-dispatch (§5; `supervisor/workers.py`, `supervisor/queue_snapshot.py`, `supervisor/task_admission.py`).
20. **A stall says where it went silent.** `supervisor_loop_stall` (closed with stacks by `supervisor_loop_stall_end`) names tick phase and lag beside off-loop `host_duty_stall` and `direct_turn_stall`; only the wedge notice speaks, while journal and `direct_turn` receipts inform the mind's judgment (which decides whether the owner hears).

22. **A host-owed round never parks.** Turns park behind a review panel only when waiting on nothing else, never right after host speech (`loop._finalize_loop_candidate`).
23. **A reviewer's tool call has a bound, and a recorder speaks only for what it collected.** Per-tool timeouts narrow to the dispatch deadline, abandoning late answers without receipts; waves whose author stopped waiting settle later from recorded seat answers at $0 without re-send (`review_native_episode.py`, `loop_tool_execution.py`, `review_pause.py`).
24. **The thread that answers workers runs only queue-bounded work.** Network and preparation run off-thread and a generation child maintains history. `Q` spans memory only except at sites named by `test_queue_lock_io_inventory.py` (which drives real admissions, parks and wakes); durable work follows release and revalidates on re-take, closing admission never fakes physical stop, and idle cancel/delivery/notice/usage duties keep claims and receipts without rereading rows (`test_cost_live_set.py`; residuals: `dispatch_choice.py`, `pool_admission.py`, §5). Observers do not queue behind heavy collection, current selection re-proves no unrelated completed history, stop-time engine access is attach-only, and no server thread starts a focus (task body, context build, wake preparation, Presence body) beyond the disclosed inline executors (`inline_call`, `inline_degraded`; `tests/test_executor_inventory.py` names every door and residual; `server_maintenance.py`, `conversation_lane.py`, `inline_carrier.py`, `tree_census.py`, `delegate_collection.py`, `sleep_custody.py`).
25. **An answer that has not arrived is a gap, never a refusal, a failure, a verdict or an owner message.** Readable sources return saved work via exact grants (stops: invariant 31). Admission locks precede `Q` and pooled attempts prove currency before publish; at author stop the seal runs once (only owner mail revises, silence/other refusals buy no round, unanswered seals set `admission_released=False` + `supervisor_ack_unavailable`, only `sealed` seals and absent never does, Presence frees its row; §6, `ouroboros/agent.py`, `supervisor/queue_transitions.py`, `ouroboros/loop_delivery.py`).

27. **One request keeps one work identity.** `move_to_project` places current work; `start_task` creates separate new work without predecessor or origin-sibling selection. The complete new-work intent is durable before emission; boot never replays it (proven unadmitted → `not_started` with retry; unknown stays held). 28. **A display never waits on money, and paid admission never waits on history.** Paid dispatch compares known spend with limits, separating in-flight, overshoot and estimates while keeping unknown cost unknown (never zero or sole veto); displays read addressed records without history imports (`ouroboros/usage_accounting.py`, `ouroboros/usage_store.py`).

### 10.2 Closed roads

Approaches rejected or retired that have no file of their own to carry the reason. Each states the road and why it stays closed; a line leaves when its fork ceases to exist.

- **Not a map of my body only for work on my own body**, because it is how I understand my capabilities, tools and accesses and so work effectively on any task: the Body Map is resident in every mode and focus (BIBLE P1).
- **Not whole books resident by mode or binding**, because what they were resident for (my capabilities, tools and accesses) is about a tenth of them, which the Map carries, while their whole weight kept Max from opening on common windows and made a start they alone crowded read as Low; the manuals are development detail I read where I touch my body (BIBLE P1).
- **Not a keyword or pattern router that decides which manual I read**, because what to read is my judgment (BIBLE P5); the host matches a literal path to the manuals `domains.toml` declares for it and prints their addresses, nothing more.
- **Not a veto on a commit for an unread chapter or an untouched manual**, because review coverage is diagnostic (BIBLE P3) and a mandatory touch buys an edit that documents nothing; the diff pulse states which owning manual a change left untouched and the reviewer judges.
- **Not tracked generated maps of the code**, such as the domain map and the facade, data-layout and frozen-contract inventories, because their sources answer on demand (`query_code` architecture facts, `scripts/check_domains.py --map`) and a tracked copy must be regenerated in every change touching its source.
- **Not grant files, per-chapter byte budgets or a no-growth rule on whole books**, because a size rule belongs where every request pays: `BIBLE.md`, the prompts and the Body Map end no larger than at their base with no stored number or reserve, while a chapter read on demand is bound by one read and splits by subject; a cap on whole books was paid by deleting their reasons (`tests/test_reference_book_budgets.py`).
- **Not a resident Pattern Register or host-written execution reflections as a privileged learning organ**, because a lesson is a change in what I understand, keep in view or can do, put where it acts; such notes are ordinary knowledge.
- **Not connectors that filter events themselves**, because filtering by the connector replaces my judgment; connectors report observed provider facts (`event.facts`, CREATING_SKILLS) and the mind decides (§12).
- **Not a separate directory of decision records**, because a reason kept beside its rule ("not X, because Y") is read where the rule is changed, on every fork; only roads without such a home are listed here.
