Ouroboros in projects and research.

Ouroboros is finding its way into other projects and research on self-developing agents. Here are examples with links to the work itself.

Download the desktop appRead the technical report

Projects adapting code and ideas

  • DPC Messenger adapts Ouroboros's agent for its messaging system, with its own model manager, context, tools, and local state. Its agent source credits Ouroboros and explains the changes.
  • offload-harness reuses Ouroboros ideas for tracking failure classes, retaining review evidence, and recording checks that were not run. Its design decision names the ideas it adopted.

Research that cites Ouroboros

  • Self-Evolving Coding Agents discusses persistent implementation changes and the distinction between a frozen benchmark snapshot and an agent that keeps developing.
  • The Last AI Built by Humans: Toward Genuine Recursive Self-Improvement discusses Ouroboros as a case of post-deployment changes to agent software.
  • RSIAgent: Autonomous Exploration for Recursive Self-improvement in New Environments cites Ouroboros in its related work on recursive self-improvement.
  • MetaRSI / RSI2: A Meta-Recursive Self-Improving System for Recursive Self-Improving Systems Themselves includes Ouroboros in its survey of evaluation domains and verification methods.
